About Linei

Linei is a workout channel that shows one simple at-home move together with the numbers from research. The character in the videos is an AI-made mannequin.

The checklists are free pages for following the move from each video day by day. The reps and duration follow the study that measured that move.

New videos are posted on Instagram @linei.fit.
